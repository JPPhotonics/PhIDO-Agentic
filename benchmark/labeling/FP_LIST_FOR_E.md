## Design implications for improvement E

1. **Mistyped tails defeat a naive type check.** Many `PERFORMS_FUNCTION` FP tails are device nouns (Light Source, Photodetector, Optical Switch, Power Splitter) that upstream PPC typing tagged as `Design_Function`, so the edge is schema-conformant. E must validate that the tail is a *genuine action/function*, not a device noun mistyped as one — or fix the typing upstream. A check that only asks "is tail Function-typed?" will pass these.

2. **The true relation is usually containment/use, not function.** Evidence quotes for these FPs say "integrated with", "connected to", "consisted of" → `CONTAINS_/USES_COMPONENT`. E should prefer a structural relation when the quote is a composition statement.

3. **Evidence is entity-pooled, not relation-specific** (root cause #3): several FPs carry quotes about unrelated topics (e.g. SiN-waveguide FPs quote cantilever beam-scanning). A relation-specific quote check at construction time would catch these.

4. **Confidence does not separate FPs** (0.8–0.99, several at 0.98–0.99): thresholding will not filter them — the fix must be structural (typing + relation-specific evidence), not a cutoff.
